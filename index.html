Freshness & Storage Tips

Keep your microgreens crisp for 10+ days

  • Refrigerate Immediately: Store between 2°C to 5°C upon delivery.
  • Keep Dry: Tuck a dry paper towel inside the box to absorb excess moisture.
  • Wash Before Serving: Do not wash prior to refrigerating; rinse only right before eating.
